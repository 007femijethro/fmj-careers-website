Our hiring approach

We aim for a thoughtful process, rather than long, drawn-out rounds. Typical stages may include:

  • Initial CV review and shortlisting
  • Conversation about fit, values and work style
  • Role-specific case or exercise (where relevant)
  • Final conversation and mutual questions
We value honest, two-way conversations at every step.
